# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

**Grid SQL** uses an Open Core model. External contributions are welcome under Apache License 2.0 for the open tree, but GenCloud must retain the legal freedom to relicense future versions and to combine community contributions with proprietary modules.

This CLA is **required** for every pull request from outside GenCloud maintainers. A lighter Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) is **not** sufficient for this project’s relicensing needs.

This document is a project policy template. It is not a substitute for advice from your counsel. If your employer owns your work product, use the Corporate CLA section below.

## Definitions

- **Project** — the Grid SQL repositories and related materials published by GenCloud under `org.genfork` / GenCloud branding.
- **Contribution** — any original work of authorship, including code, documentation, tests, configuration, and design materials, that you intentionally submit to the Project (pull request, patch, issue attachment, or other channel designated by maintainers).
- **You** / **Contributor** — the copyright owner submitting the Contribution, or an authorized representative of that owner.
- **GenCloud** — GenCloud / org.genfork, copyright holder named in `NOTICE`.

### How individuals accept

Open a pull request and include in the PR description (or a maintainers-requested comment):

```text
I have read and agree to the Grid SQL Contributor License Agreement (CLA.md).
I am the sole copyright owner of my Contribution, or I have authority to bind the copyright owner.
```

Maintainers may ask You to restate acceptance if the PR is large, if authorship is unclear, or if Your employer must sign the Corporate CLA.

## Corporate Contributor License Agreement

If employees or contractors of a company (“**Corporation**”) will contribute, an authorized representative of the Corporation must agree as follows.

By authorizing Contributions from its employees and contractors, the Corporation:

1. Agrees to the same grant, assignment, fallback license, patent license, and representations as in the Individual CLA, on behalf of the Corporation as copyright owner;
2. Designates which employees/contractors may submit on its behalf (or states “all employees and contractors contributing to Grid SQL”);
3. Agrees that GenCloud may rely on this Corporate CLA for all future Contributions from those designated persons until the Corporation revokes in writing (revocation does not affect prior Contributions).

### How corporations accept

Email maintainers (or attach to the first PR) a statement signed by an authorized representative:

```text
Corporate CLA acceptance — Grid SQL (CLA.md)

Corporation legal name: _______________________________
Jurisdiction / address: ________________________________
Authorized signatory name / title: _____________________
Email: ________________________________________________
Employees/contractors covered: _________________________

On behalf of the Corporation, I have read and agree to the
Corporate Contributor License Agreement in CLA.md.

Signature / date: ______________________________________
```

## Maintainers (GenCloud)

Contributions authored solely by GenCloud maintainers in the course of their work for GenCloud are already owned by GenCloud and do not require a separate external CLA acceptance.

## Out of scope

- Trivial typo fixes that contain no copyrightable expression may be accepted at maintainers’ discretion without a full CLA restatement; when in doubt, CLA acceptance is still required.
- Third-party code under a different license must not be pasted into the tree without an explicit maintainer decision and compatible licensing.
